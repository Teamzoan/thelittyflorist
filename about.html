My Story

Welcome! I’m Zoe, and I am so glad you’ve found your way to my floral corner of the world.

At The Litty Florist, I believe that flowers are more than just beautiful arrangements, they are a language of emotion. My journey with petals and stems began nearly twenty years ago, and since then, I have dedicated my heart to the "Litty" aesthetic: a signature blend of high-end luxury and raw, natural beauty.

Whether I’m crafting a symbolic bouquet for Her Majesty the Queen, designing a whimsical headpiece for Royal Ascot, or hand-selecting stems for a local wedding, my goal is always the same: to tell a unique story. I love the challenge of taking a fleeting moment and turning it into an artisan masterpiece.

Based here in beautiful St Ives, Cambridgeshire, I treat every petal with an artisan’s touch. To me, floristry is about capturing the wildness of nature and the elegance of a special occasion, all in one hand-tied bundle.

With love,

Zoe

My Story

At The Litty Florist, we believe that flowers are more than just arrangements—they are a language of emotion. Based in St Ives - Cambridgeshire, our focuses is on the "Litty" aesthetic: a blend of high-end luxury and raw, natural beauty.

Every stem is hand-selected, and every bouquet is curated to tell a unique story. Whether it's a grand wedding installation or a simple weekly subscription, we bring an artisan touch to every petal.

Zoe - The Litty Florist

Landmark Achievements

July 2019

By Appointment of Her Majesty

Commissioned to create a bespoke hand-tied bouquet for Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II during her historic visit to the NIAB Centenary. The arrangement featured a symbolic blend of Chrysanthemums, Roses, and Wheat.

Royal Commission
June 2015

Royal Ascot Winner

Named an Interflora Gold Member winner for the "Fit for a Royal Occasion" challenge. The winning creation was a surrealist floral hat inspired by the 150th anniversary of Alice in Wonderland, featuring Cymbidium orchids and tortured willow.

Interflora Gold Winner
2014 & 2016

RHS Chelsea Flower Show

Two-time Heat Finalist for the prestigious Young Florist of the Year award. Competing at this level places a designer among the technical and creative elite of the UK floral industry.

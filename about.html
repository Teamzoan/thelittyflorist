The Story

At The Litty Florist, we believe that flowers are more than just arrangements—they are a language of emotion. Based in St Ives - Cambridgeshire, our focuses is on the "Litty" aesthetic: a blend of high-end luxury and raw, natural beauty.

Every stem is hand-selected, and every bouquet is curated to tell a unique story. Whether it's a grand wedding installation or a bespoke arrangement, we bring an artisan touch to every petal.

Zoe - The Litty Florist

Landmark Achievements

July 2019

By Appointment of Her Majesty

Commissioned to create a bespoke hand-tied bouquet for Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II during her visit to NIAB. The arrangement featured a symbolic blend of Chrysanthemums, Roses, and Wheat, specifically designed to be small enough to be held in one hand.

Royal Commission
June 2015

Royal Ascot Winner

Named an Interflora Gold Member winner for the "Fit for a Royal Occasion" challenge. The surrealist floral hat was inspired by the 150th anniversary of Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, featuring tortured willow to mimic the Mad Hatter.

Interflora Gold Winner
2014

RHS Chelsea Flower Show

Heat Finalist for the Young Florist of the Year award. Competing at this level demonstrates professional mastery and identifies the "very best floristry talent" in the country.
